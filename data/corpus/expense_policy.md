# Travel and Expense Policy — Lumen Freight Ltd.

## Submitting expenses
- Submit expenses in **ExpenseFlow** within **30 days** of the expense date.
- Receipts are required for any expense above **UAH 200**.

## Approval limits
- Expenses up to **UAH 10,000** are approved by your line manager.
- Expenses above UAH 10,000 require approval from the **Finance Director**.

## Domestic business travel
- Meal per diem: **UAH 600 per day**.
- Hotel limit in Kyiv and Lviv: **UAH 3,500 per night**.
- Taxis are reimbursed only for trips after **22:00** or when carrying heavy equipment or luggage.

## Not reimbursed
- Alcohol is **not reimbursed** under any circumstances, including client meals.
- Traffic and parking fines.
