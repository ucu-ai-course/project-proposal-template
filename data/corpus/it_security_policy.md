# IT Security Policy — Lumen Freight Ltd.

## Accounts
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for all company accounts.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

## Devices
- A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT Service Desk (**extension 4400**) within **1 hour** of discovery.
- USB storage devices are **blocked** on all company laptops.

## Phishing
- Report suspicious emails with the **"Report phishing"** button in your mail client, or forward them to **security@lumenfreight.example**.

## Data classification
- Company information has four levels: **Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted**.
- Confidential and Restricted information must not be pasted into external AI tools or shared outside the company.
