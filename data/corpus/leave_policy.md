# Leave Policy — Lumen Freight Ltd.

## Annual leave
- Every full-time employee is entitled to **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; after that they expire.
- Leave longer than 3 working days must be requested in **HRPortal** at least **10 working days** in advance and approved by your line manager.

## Sick leave
- Notify your line manager **before 10:00** on the first day of absence.
- A doctor's note is required if you are absent for **more than 3 consecutive days**.

## Parental leave
- Parental leave is granted in accordance with national law. Contact HR (hr@lumenfreight.example) for details on your situation.

## Public holidays
- The company follows the national public holiday calendar.
