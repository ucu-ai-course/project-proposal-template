# Onboarding Guide — Lumen Freight Ltd.

- On your first day, come to reception at **09:30**.
- Pick up your laptop from the IT Service Desk on day 1. Your account will be ready.
- Every new employee is assigned an **onboarding buddy** from their team.
- Mandatory **security awareness training** must be completed within the first **14 days**.
- The **probation period** is **3 months**.
- Useful contacts: HR — hr@lumenfreight.example; IT Service Desk — extension 4400.
