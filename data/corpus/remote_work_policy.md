# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy — Lumen Freight Ltd.

- The company works in a **hybrid** model. Employees must be in the office at least **2 days per week**. **Tuesday and Thursday** are team days.
- Working fully remotely requires written approval from your **department director**.
- Employees may work from abroad for up to **30 calendar days per year**, with prior approval from HR.
- Each employee receives a one-time **home office equipment allowance of UAH 6,000**, claimed through ExpenseFlow.
- When working outside the office, you must connect to company systems through the **company VPN**.
